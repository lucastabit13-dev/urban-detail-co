How SiO2 Hydrophobic Barriers Function

Unlike traditional car soaps that simply float away surface dust, SiO2 ceramic foam contains micro-emulsified silica polymers. As the soapy foam is applied and rinsed off, these polymers leave behind an invisible, slick shield that increases surface tension.

HOME / KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Find clear answers regarding our services, ceramic technology, and vehicle prep guidelines.

Is the $100 Full Detail package a flat rate for all vehicle conditions?

The $100 Full Detail is structured as a standard maintenance detail. If a vehicle requires extreme interior cleaning—such as heavy pet hair extraction, severe mud accumulation, deep carpet staining, or biohazard cleanup—the price may vary and an adjusted estimate will be provided upon initial vehicle inspection.

What is included in the $100 Full Detail package?

The $100 Full Detail combines our $50 Exterior Wash and $60 Interior Cleaning into one complete package (saving $10). It covers ceramic foam wash, pressure rinse, microfiber towel hand drying, exterior & interior glass polish, vacuuming, dash & console cleanup, and door jamb wipes.

Do I need to prep or clear out my car before my appointment?

Yes! Please follow our 4-step Vehicle Prep Tour: remove excess bulky debris, personal items, child seats, and valuables prior to your slot. Clearing your car in advance helps us maintain our fast turnaround times and flat-rate pricing.

What are the benefits of Ceramic Soap?

Ceramic soap contains SiO2 silica polymers. As we wash your vehicle, it leaves behind a sleek hydrophobic protective layer that repels water, prevents road film buildup, and yields an elevated glossy finish.

How long does a detailing session take?

An Exterior Wash ($50) takes approximately 45 minutes. An Interior Cleaning ($60) takes around 60 minutes. The Full Detail Package ($100) takes roughly 1.5 to 2 hours.
